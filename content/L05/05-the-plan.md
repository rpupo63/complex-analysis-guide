<ol>
<li><b>A comparison lemma</b> (@lem:comparison), for our $u$: the harmonic $L^2$ limit of the $\class{ntn-Un}{U_n}$, with $\nabla \class{ntn-Un}{U_n}\to\nabla u$ and $\class{ntn-Un}{U_n}=g$ on $\class{ntn-dOm}{\partial\Omega}$. If $v$ is continuous on $\overline\Omega$, harmonic in $\Omega$, and $v\ge g$ on $\class{ntn-dOm}{\partial\Omega}$, then $v\ge u$. This is a maximum principle for $v-u$ whose boundary inequality is checked against $g$, the boundary values of the $\class{ntn-Un}{U_n}$, because those of $u$ are not yet known to exist.</li>
<li><b>Barriers</b> (@sec:v11). At each $p\in\class{ntn-dOm}{\partial\Omega}$, explicit harmonic functions $w\ge0$: of size about $1$ at $p$, but as large as we like on the rest of $\class{ntn-dOm}{\partial\Omega}$. Then $\class{ntn-vpm}{v_\pm}:=g(p)\pm(\text{small}+k\,w)$ squeeze $u$ at $p$ (@sec:v12).</li>
</ol>
<p>The lemma is where the energy works. The barrier is a logarithm: the potential of a point charge placed just outside $\Omega$ ([logarithmic potential](apx:E6)).</p>
