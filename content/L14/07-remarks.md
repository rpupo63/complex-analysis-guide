::: remark {#rem:extremal-property name="an extremal property"}
Steps 2, 3 and 5 of the proof show more: among all injective holomorphic $F:\Omega\to D(0,1)$ with $F(z_0)=0$, the Riemann map has the largest $|F'(z_0)|$. Indeed $F\circ f^{-1}$ maps the disk into itself and $0$ to $0$, so $|F'(z_0)|\le f'(z_0)$ by the Schwarz lemma. Koebe and Fejér–Riesz turned this around: their proof takes a maximizer of $|F'(z_0)|$ (it exists by Montel and Hurwitz) and shows it is onto by step 5. That proof needs no Dirichlet problem (@rem:the-general-theorem).
:::
