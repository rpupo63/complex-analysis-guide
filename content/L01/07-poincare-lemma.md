::: fact {#fact:poincare-lemma-on-a-disk name="Poincaré lemma on a disk" status=cited}
Let $D$ be an open disk and $W=(W_1,W_2)$ a $C^1$ vector field on $D$ with $\class{ntn-divc}{\operatorname{curl}}W=0$. Then $W$ is a gradient: $W=\nabla\varphi$ for some $\varphi:D\to\R$, unique up to an additive constant.
:::
<p>The slides call this a standard fact; a complete proof is in [the Poincaré lemma](apx:A7). Not to be confused with Poincaré's <em>inequality</em> (@thm:poincares-inequality), a different result by the same mathematician. The disk matters: @sec:x14 shows the lemma fails on $\mathbb C\setminus\{0\}$.</p>
