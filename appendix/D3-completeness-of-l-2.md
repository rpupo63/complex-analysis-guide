<div class="env"><span class="hd">Theorem.</span> Every Cauchy sequence in $L^2(\Omega)$ converges in $L^2(\Omega)$ to some $f\in L^2(\Omega)$.</div>
<p>This is the step that produces the limit function $u$ from nothing but estimates. It needs the Lebesgue integral: with Riemann-integrable functions alone, the limit may fail to exist in the space. The slides use it without comment in "call their limits $u,p,q$".</p>
