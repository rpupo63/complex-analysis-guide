title: Sobolev spaces $\class{ntn-H1}{H^1}$, $\class{ntn-H1}{H^1_0}$ and the trace
status:
- defined
- cited
---
<p>$\class{ntn-H1}{H^1}(\Omega)$ is the set of $u\in L^2(\Omega)$ whose weak derivatives $\partial_xu,\partial_yu$ (@def:weak-derivative) exist and lie in $L^2(\Omega)$. $\class{ntn-H1}{H^1_0}(\Omega)$ is the set of limits, in the sense of item 1 of @thm:existence-of-a-weak-solution (function and gradient both converge in $L^2$), of piecewise smooth functions vanishing on $\class{ntn-dOm}{\partial\Omega}$. Item 3 of that theorem says exactly $u-\class{ntn-Un}{U_0}\in \class{ntn-H1}{H^1_0}(\Omega)$.</p>
<p>The <em>trace theorem</em> (cited) says that, for reasonable domains, restriction to $\class{ntn-dOm}{\partial\Omega}$ extends from smooth functions to a well-defined map on $\class{ntn-H1}{H^1}(\Omega)$, whose kernel is $\class{ntn-H1}{H^1_0}(\Omega)$. So $u-\class{ntn-Un}{U_0}\in \class{ntn-H1}{H^1_0}$ means "$u$ has the same boundary trace as $\class{ntn-Un}{U_0}$", namely $g$.</p>
