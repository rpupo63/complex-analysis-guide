title: Affine sets
status:
- defined
---
<p>A subset $S$ of a vector space is <em>affine</em> if $\lambda U+(1-\lambda)V\in S$ whenever $U,V\in S$ and $\lambda\in\R$. A nonempty affine set is a translate of a linear subspace: fixing any $U_*\in S$, $S=U_*+\{U-U_*:U\in S\}$, and the set of differences is linear. For $S=\class{ntn-Vg}{V^g_\eps}$ the difference space is $\class{ntn-Vz}{V^0_\eps}$, as @prop:v-g-eps-is-affine-not and [#(d)](@prop:the-space-v-0-eps) show. A line not through the origin is the basic picture.</p>
