title: Barriers and regular boundary points
status:
- context
---
<p>A <em>barrier</em> at $p\in\class{ntn-dOm}{\partial\Omega}$ is a function harmonic (or superharmonic) in $\Omega$, continuous on $\overline\Omega$, positive on $\overline\Omega$ except at $p$ where it is smallest. A boundary point with a barrier is called <em>regular</em>: at such points every Dirichlet solution takes its boundary value continuously, by an argument like @sec:v12.</p>
<p>For a union of squares every boundary point is regular, thanks to the empty square of @lem:an-empty-coarse-square-at-p. More generally, an <em>exterior cone</em> or exterior disk at $p$ is enough. Not every domain qualifies: a punctured disk (the boundary point $0$ is isolated) admits no solution with $u(0)$ prescribed independently of the outer circle, and Lebesgue's "spike" in three dimensions has an irregular tip. In the plane, any boundary point lying on a nontrivial connected piece of the boundary is regular.</p>
