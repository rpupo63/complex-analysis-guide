<p>@rem:beyond-grids says the comparison lemma is a grid-free statement in disguise. In Sobolev language ([D.6](apx:D6)) it reads: <em>if $u\in \class{ntn-H1}{H^1}(\Omega)$ is weakly harmonic and $(u-v)^+\in \class{ntn-H1}{H^1_0}(\Omega)$, then $u\le v$ almost everywhere.</em> The proof is the one of @sec:v9–@sec:v10: test the weak equation with $(u-v-\sigma)^+$, whose energy then vanishes. The condition $(u-v)^+\in \class{ntn-H1}{H^1_0}$ is "$u\le v$ on $\class{ntn-dOm}{\partial\Omega}$ in the trace sense", exactly what the $\class{ntn-Un}{U_n}$ supplied through $\class{ntn-Un}{U_n}=g\le v$. This is the starting point of the $L^2$ theory of elliptic equations with variable coefficients, where no mean value property is available.</p>
