<div class="env"><span class="hd">Theorem.</span> If $\iint\!\iint|F(\xi,\zeta)|\,dA(\xi)\,dA(\zeta)\lt\infty$, then the double integral of $F$ can be computed as an iterated integral in either order, with the same result. The same holds for a continuous integrand on a bounded region split into $t$ and $\theta$.</div>
<p>Used to split disk integrals into circles (@lem:averaging) and to swap the order of two convolutions (@lem:the-order-of-two-smoothings-does).</p>
