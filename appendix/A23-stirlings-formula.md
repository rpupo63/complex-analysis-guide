<div class="env"><span class="hd">Theorem (cited).</span> As $K\to\infty$,
\[ \log K!=K\log K-K+\tfrac12\log(2\pi K)+O\big(\tfrac1K\big),\qquad\text{equivalently}\qquad K!\sim\sqrt{2\pi K}\Big(\frac Ke\Big)^K. \]</div>
<p>The main term $K\log K-K$ is $\int_1^K\log t\,dt$ up to a constant: compare the sum $\log K!=\sum_{k\le K}\log k$ with the integral. The constant $\sqrt{2\pi}$ can be found from Wallis's product, or from $\Gamma(\frac12)=\sqrt\pi$ (@cor:the-value-at-frac12-and-the). Used in @sec:j7, and in the outline of @lem:lemma-a ([E.19](apx:E19)).</p>
