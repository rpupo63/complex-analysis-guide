<p>The continuous version of @lem:among-all-is-the-unique: the harmonic function with boundary values $g$ minimizes $\iint|\nabla v|^2$ among all $v$ with those boundary values. Riemann used it in the 1850s by assuming a minimizer exists. Weierstrass showed that an energy-type functional can have a positive infimum that no admissible function attains, so the assumption needed proof. Hilbert rehabilitated the principle around 1900.</p>
<p>The slides avoid the gap: they never assume a minimizer. They build the minimizers $\class{ntn-Un}{U_n}$ over finite-dimensional sets, where existence is linear algebra, and obtain $u$ as a limit.</p>
