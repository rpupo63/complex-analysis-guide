<div class="env"><span class="hd">Theorem (Schwarz / Clairaut).</span> If $h$ is $C^2$ on an open set, then $h_{xy}=h_{yx}$ there: the order of differentiation does not matter.</div>
<p>Without continuity of the second derivatives this can fail; the standard counterexample is $h=xy\frac{x^2-y^2}{x^2+y^2}$ at the origin. @prop:if-satisfy-the-cauchy-riemann needs the theorem to cancel $v_{yx}$ against $v_{xy}$.</p>
