Suppose that polynomials $p_n$ converge uniformly on the unit circle to a function $g$. Prove that $(p_n)$ converges uniformly on the closed disk $\{|z|\le 1\}$. Show also that $g(z)=\bar z$ is not such a limit.
<details class="sol"><summary>Hint</summary>
On the closed disk, $|p_n-p_m|$ is at most its maximum on the circle (@lem:maximum-modulus). For $\bar z$, pass to the limit in $\oint p_n\,dz=0$.
</details>
<details class="sol"><summary>Solution</summary>
<ol>
<li><em>Uniform Cauchy on the disk.</em> Each difference $p_n-p_m$ is a polynomial, hence holomorphic near $\{|z|\le1\}$. By @lem:maximum-modulus, $|p_n(z)-p_m(z)|\le\max_{|z|=1}|p_n-p_m|$ for every $|z|\le1$. Uniform convergence on the circle means the right side tends to $0$ as $n,m\to\infty$. So $(p_n)$ is uniformly Cauchy on the closed disk, and therefore converges uniformly there ([A.17](apx:A17)).</li>
<li><em>$\bar z$ is not such a limit.</em> On $|z|=1$, $\bar z=1/z$. Suppose polynomials $p_n$ converge uniformly to $\bar z$ on the circle. Every polynomial is entire, so $\oint_{|z|=1}p_n\,dz=0$ by @thm:cauchys-theorem. On the other hand $\oint_{|z|=1}dz/z=2\pi i$ (@cor:the-residue-theorem-point-charge-form). The difference of the integrals satisfies
\[ \Big|\oint_{|z|=1}(p_n(z)-\bar z)\,dz\Big|\le 2\pi\max_{|z|=1}|p_n-\bar z| \]
by the [ML estimate](apx:A25), and the right side tends to $0$. So $0=2\pi i$, a contradiction.</li>
</ol>
</details>
